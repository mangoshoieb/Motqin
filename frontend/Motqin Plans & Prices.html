Audience 1

Students

طلاب الثانوية العامة والبكالوريا

Second and third secondary, Thanaweya Amma and the Egyptian Baccalaureate. Study sessions and reviews stay unlimited on every plan, Free included. Plans differ on AI, the student's own content, the blocker and mentors.

Audience 2

Teachers

مدرسين

Teachers who track their groups in Motqin and send results to parents on WhatsApp. Parent messages are the real cost, so each plan carries about 2 reports per student a month.

Audience 3

Parents & mentors

أولياء أمور ومرشدين

Parents and mentors who follow students. Only one feature is paid today, how many students one account follows, so the plans differ by that number. Following one child stays free.
