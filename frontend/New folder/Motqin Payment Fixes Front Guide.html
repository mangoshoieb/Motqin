General noteschanged

A student can no longer buy a plan that starts later. While a paid plan is running, the only thing they can buy is an upgrade, paying the difference for the days left. After the plan ends, they can buy any plan.

What the student can buy
Student's planCan buyoperationStarts
Free (or Guest after sign-up)Any paid planPurchaseNow
Paid, still runningA higher plan with the same term that keeps every main feature they have, or their own plan with a main feature added backUpgradeNow, ends with the current plan
Paid, still runningAnything else: the same plan again, a lower plan, a different termrefused: upgrade_only—
Paid, ended, in the 3 grace days (current.inGrace)The same planRenewalActive now; its days count from the old end date
Paid, ended, in the 3 grace daysAny other planPurchaseNow

An upgrade costs the price-list difference for the days left, and coupons never apply to it.

Testing the payment fixes

On the web, signed in as a student with a Paymob test card for top-ups. These are the checks from the 21–23 Nov run to repeat after the front changes.

What you need
ItemValue

StudentA new student account (the first-purchase coupon works once).
CouponsMade with POST /api/admin/coupons: one for everything, one for Motqin Pro only, one not allowed on customised plans, one expired.

Top-ups use Paymob's test keys, so no real money moves.

  1. Plans page
  2. On Free, apply the Motqin-Pro-only coupon

  3. Apply the whole-plan-only coupon, then untick a main feature

  4. Checkout and coupons
  5. Checkout with the expired coupon

  6. Buy Plus with the first-purchase coupon, short of balance

  7. Plan running
  8. Plans page with Plus running

  10. Upgrade to Motqin with a coupon typed in

  12. In the grace days (move the end date into the past on the dev DB)

    Banner جدّد الآن حتى لا تتوقف مزاياك، أو اختر أي باقة أخرى; every card buyable. Renewing shows the hint تبدأ الآن، وتُحسب من تاريخ انتهاء باقتك السابقة.

  14. Wallet
  15. Start a top-up and close Paymob without paying; wait past its hour
