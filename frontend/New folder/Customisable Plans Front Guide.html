Customisable Plans Front Guide

اشتراكك على كيفك: a student can take main features off a bundle and pay less. This guide has only the endpoints that are new or changed for it, plus the two changes that came with it: coupons that can skip customised plans, and mentors whose students are paused when their plan no longer covers them. Everything else stays as in the Payments and Mentors guides.

Sends groups with every preview and the purchase. Shows the price in steps: fullPrice, minus featuresDiscount (خصم المميزات المحذوفة), minus the coupon's discountAmount, = amountDue. On couponError: coupon_not_for_customized, offer اشترك في الباقة كاملة, which previews again with groups: null.

BundlesController · /api/bundles

Bundles (plans)

New fields that tell the app which main features a student may take off, what each is worth, and what's left without it.

GET/api/bundles?audience=new fieldsanyone

Same query and order as before. Each plan now says whether it can be customised and groups its features by main feature. GET /api/bundles/{id} returns the same new fields.

Lookups and dependencies
POST /api/subscription/previewthe exact price for a choice, with a coupon.
  • Live price while the student switches: add up discountPercent of every switched-off group, then ceil(price × (100 − total) / 100), and at least 1 EGP below price once anything is off. S2, 1 month: blocker off 153 · Mentor off 162 · planner off 135 · only the planner kept 81.

MentorshipController · /api/mentorship · the mentor's side

Check it yourself

Manual test

The run that produced the bodies above, in order. Four registered accounts: Ahmed and Bassem (students), Sara (mentor), Camilia (a student on Free), and an admin for the coupons. A 100% voucher batch pays for the purchases.

  1. Plans and prices

  2. GET /api/bundles?audience=Student

    S2-1M: customizable true, blocker 15% · saving 26, Mentor 10% · saving 17, BlockerSessions limitIfLeftOut 15, Mentees 3 · accessIfLeftOut Off.

  3. Camilia previews S2 with groups: ["StudyPlanner"] and a coupon with allowOnCustomized: false.

    listPrice 81, couponError coupon_not_for_customized. The same coupon with groups: null: amountDue 161.10.

  4. Camilia previews with groups: [], with ["TrackingTables"], and M1 with ["Mentor"].

    invalid_groups, invalid_groups, bundle_not_customizable.

  5. Buying without a feature

  6. Ahmed buys S2 with ["StudyPlanner","SpacedRepetition","Mentor"] and a voucher, then GET /api/subscription/me.

    listPrice 153, amountCharged 0. BlockerSessions 15, included false; BlockerSchedules 1; AiImageQuestion 100, included true.

  7. Ahmed previews: groups: null · the same groups with a 10% coupon · without Mentor · blocker in, Mentor out · S2-6M.

    Upgrade 26 · Renewal 137.70 · Downgrade 135 · Downgrade 162 · Scheduled.

  8. Bassem buys S2 without Mentor. Camilia asks Bassem to mentor her; Bassem accepts.

    Bassem's Mentees Off, included false; accept 403 not_in_plan.

  9. Paused mentors

  10. Ahmed (Free) links with Sara (Free) before the catalogue update, and gives her performance and the blocker lock. Apply the catalogue.

    Sara's list: Ahmed paused. Ahmed's blockerLock.locked false. Sara opening Ahmed or approving: 403 mentorship_paused. Ahmed taking performance back: applied at once.

  11. Sara buys M1, then Bassem and Camilia link with her.

    All three served; Ahmed's blocker locked again.

  12. Sara buys S1 for 6 months (Scheduled). Move M1's end into the past so S1 starts.

    S1 has 1 mentee: Ahmed (accepted first) served, Bassem and Camilia paused.

  13. Move S1's end 4 days into the past (past the 3 grace days).

    Sara on Free, all three paused. Camilia unlinks: Ended at once.

  14. Sara buys M1 again.

    Ahmed and Bassem served, Ahmed's lock applies again.

Moving a plan's dates needs the database; on the dev server, ask the backend to do it for a test account.
